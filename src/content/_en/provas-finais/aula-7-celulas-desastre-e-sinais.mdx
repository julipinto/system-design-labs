<h3 class="sec">The problem of block 7</h3>

<p>The store is now a stack with compartments, and three questions went unanswered: what to do with a poison pill that takes everything down, what to do when all of SP goes down, and how to know, before Lena complains, that something is slow.</p>

<HowItWorks>
<ol class="flow steps">
  <li><strong>Divide the stack (module 26).</strong> The whole stack becomes <strong>4 cells</strong>: <code>celula-sp</code> (33%), <code>celula-rio</code> (15%), <code>celula-recife</code> (12%) and <code>celula-mkt</code> (40%). <code>edge-loja</code> reads the <code>cliente_id</code> on the map and routes. The <code>mkt-01</code> poison pill in 4 min makes <strong>7,200 × 2,880</strong> lost attempts, single stack against cells. Each cell has a <strong>passive</strong> one, the <em>shuffle</em> of 2 in 10 limits the downed pair to 1 ÷ 45 = 2.2% of customers, and the 2027 plan goes from <strong>41 to 53 vCPU</strong>. The order is <strong>1060</strong>. <a href={L('cell-based')}>Cell-Based Architecture</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Come back from the disaster (27).</strong> Cells help, but a region can still go down. The audit finds <strong>12 links and 5 SPoFs</strong>. When SP goes down, MTTD of 30 s + MTTR of 35 s give an <strong>RTO of 65 s</strong>, and the expected RPO of <code>celula-sp</code> is <strong>2.97</strong> orders. The strategy is chosen by the cost of the failure: active-active, active-passive, <em>pilot light</em> or backup, with losses from R$ 2.28 million (backup only) to R$ 28 thousand (active-active). Order <strong>1061</strong> showed up on two orders: the promoted database's sequence reused the last replicated value. <a href={L('disaster-recovery')}>Single Point of Failure and Disaster Recovery</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Know (28).</strong> Coming back fast only matters if someone notices the outage. The <strong>three signals</strong> (logs, metrics and traces) tied together by the <code>traceparent</code> show <strong>1063</strong> at 1.9 s, where normal is 275 ms. The alert wakes you by symptom, the SLI becomes a <strong>99.9% SLO</strong> (and a 99.5% SLA), the budget is <strong>43.2 min</strong> and a <em>burn rate</em> of <strong>14.4×</strong> consumes 2% in 1 h. USE looks at the resource, RED at the service and the four signals at what the user feels. <a href={L('observabilidade')}>Observability and Monitoring</a>.</li>
</ol>
</HowItWorks>

<h3 class="sec">Take a piece out: observability</h3>

<p>Without observability, the MTTD goes back from <strong>30 s to 60 min</strong>, and Lena waits <strong>1.9 s</strong> with nobody knowing where. Without the passive cell and DR, SP goes down with an RTO of <strong>~48 min</strong> and R$ <strong>2.28 million</strong> lost. Without the router per cell, the poison pill takes down the <strong>3 pods</strong> of <code>pedidos</code> in 150 ms. Modules 26, 27 and 28 warned about it.</p>

<h3 class="sec">What the store does not have (on purpose)</h3>

<p>The drawing ends here, and four chapters were left out. The store <strong>does not have</strong> security beyond the JWT (secrets, identity and LGPD), nor <strong>cost</strong> (the <em>FinOps</em> of the 53 vCPU bill), nor <strong>analytics and search</strong> (<em>data lake</em> and text index), nor <strong>machine learning</strong> (the recommendations). Each one is a new chapter.</p>
