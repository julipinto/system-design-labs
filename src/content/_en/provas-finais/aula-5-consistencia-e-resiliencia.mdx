<h3 class="sec">The problem of block 5</h3>

<p>Spread-out data and slow dependencies call for two families of answers. One makes sure the data stays <strong>right</strong> (modules 18 to 20). The other makes sure the store <strong>keeps selling</strong> when a neighbor fails (21). The order matters: resilience uses the first three.</p>

<HowItWorks>
<ol class="flow steps">
  <li><strong>Read from another model (module 18).</strong> The "my orders" screen now reads from <code>meus-pedidos</code>, a model built for reading. The <strong>outbox</strong> writes the event in the same transaction as the order, and the projector updates the model at 100 ev/s. Lena's document is <strong>1,787 B</strong>, and the report drops from <strong>235 ms to 0.021 ms</strong>. The order is <strong>1056</strong>. <a href={L('cqrs')}>CQRS</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Compensate (19).</strong> Davi's payment was approved and stock hit zero (<code>s-1057</code>): with no global <code>ROLLBACK</code>, the saga <strong>compensates</strong> with <code>estorno-c-7732</code>. Orchestrated, a conductor decides; choreographed, each service reacts to events. With 10% failure on stock and 5% on payment, success is <strong>85.5%</strong>, and the <em>backoff</em> waits <strong>1 · 2 · 4 · 8 · 16 s</strong>. The order is <strong>1057</strong>. <a href={L('saga')}>Saga Pattern</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Store the facts (20).</strong> In <em>event sourcing</em>, the state of <code>pedido-1047</code> is the result of replaying the stream's events. Without a <em>snapshot</em> the rehydration takes ~<strong>1,800 ms</strong>; with it, ~<strong>13 ms</strong>. A full <em>replay</em> takes <strong>2 h 47 min</strong>. <a href={L('event-sourcing')}>Event Sourcing</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Fail small and fast (21).</strong> With the data right, what is left is surviving PSP-A at 8 s. The PSP <em>timeout</em> drops to <strong>1 s</strong> (the critical one is 50 ÷ 30 = <strong>1.67 s</strong>), retry uses <em>full jitter</em> (without a rule, 3 layers of 3 attempts add up to <strong>27</strong> calls), the <em>breaker</em> opens in <strong>1.3 s</strong> and PSP-B takes over. The outbound <em>bulkheads</em> split the pool into <strong>50/30/20</strong>, and the <code>Idempotency-Key</code> keeps retry from duplicating. The order is <strong>1058</strong>. <a href={L('resiliencia')}>Resilience Patterns</a>.</li>
</ol>
</HowItWorks>

<p>The chain of deadlines closes the reasoning: <strong>5 → 3 → 2.8 → 2.5 → 2.0 → 1.0 s</strong>. Each layer waits less than the one above it, and the last hop, the PSP, has the shortest deadline.</p>
