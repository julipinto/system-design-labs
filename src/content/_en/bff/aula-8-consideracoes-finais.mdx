<p class="ponte">In lesson 7 each channel got its own metrics, and the app's 2% error rate showed up. With all of this built, the question left is whether your store needs it. The honest answer, sometimes, is no.</p>

<OneLiner>A BFF is worth it when different channels ask for different things and there is one team per channel. With a single channel, a gateway with simple composition is usually enough.</OneLiner>

<p>What gets confused most is the BFF and the gateway, and the difference is one of nature:</p>

<HowItWorks>
<ol class="flow steps">
  <li>The <strong>gateway</strong> is generic infrastructure: it routes, checks the JWT and limits the bot. It serves everyone the same way.</li>
  <li>The <strong>BFF</strong> is product code: it composes, adapts and decides the fallback for <strong>one</strong> screen. It belongs to the frontend team.</li>
  <li>The path of an app call is <code>app → gw-loja → bff-app → services</code>.</li>
  <li>The gateway looks at the front door, the BFF looks at the screen, and the services take care of the rules.</li>
</ol>
</HowItWorks>

<WhyItMatters>
  <li><strong>One backend per frontend.</strong> Each screen asks for what it needs and receives only that.</li>
  <li><strong>The caveat:</strong> too many BFFs duplicate logic. The business rule stays in the service.</li>
  <li><strong>The cost:</strong> more pieces to publish, measure and protect. The BFF only pays off when the difference between channels is real.</li>
</WhyItMatters>

  <h4 class="deep-h">And GraphQL?</h4>

It can live inside a BFF, or take its place when the clients are many and unpredictable. The choice weighs the cost of maintaining the schema against the cost of maintaining one BFF per channel.</p>

<p class="gancho">Between the BFF and the services, and between the services themselves, each call needs timeout, retry, encryption and metrics, and each team is writing that its own way, in every language. What's left is whether this can go into the network, once: that's the subject of module 10, Service Mesh.</p>
