<p class="ponte">In the last lesson the state machine took 1057 from <code>RESERVANDO</code> to <code>COMPENSANDO</code> and then to <code>ESTORNANDO</code>. What's left is to see <strong>what happens inside those states</strong>: which compensations run, in what order, and what to do when the undo itself fails.</p>

<OneLiner>Every action has an <strong>undo</strong> that can be <strong>repeated</strong>, and the saga fires them in <strong>reverse order</strong> of what was already done. The compensation has to be <strong>idempotent</strong> (the <code>estorno-c-7732</code> refund repeated refunds once) and <strong>repeatable</strong> until it passes. And sometimes it fails: then the saga goes to <code>INTERVENCAO</code> and calls a person.</OneLiner>

<h3 class="sec">What if it goes wrong (a): the refund fails</h3>

<p>The compensation fails too. The PSP answers <code>503</code> to <code>EstornarCobranca c-7732</code>. The saga tries again with <em>backoff</em>: it waits <strong>1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 s</strong> (5 retries, sum <strong>31 s</strong>). If the fifth retry also fails, the saga goes to <strong><code>INTERVENCAO</code></strong>, with an alert and all the data (saga <code>s-1057</code>, charge <code>c-7732</code>, R$ 58.00, <code>sem_estoque</code>) for a person to sort out. Since the key is <code>estorno-c-7732</code>, none of the 6 attempts refunds twice if the PSP comes back in the middle.</p>

<h3 class="sec">What if it goes wrong (b): what has no undo</h3>

<p>The "order confirmed" email already went out: the compensation is <strong>another email</strong>. The fee the PSP charged for the Pix may not come back (Microsoft: canceling a reservation "might not entitle the customer to a complete refund"). Compensating undoes the business effect, it doesn't erase history.</p>

<ol class="flow steps">
  <li><strong>The canceled saga can come back to life.</strong> The compensation replies (<code>RELEASE_STOCK</code>, <code>CANCEL_ORDER</code>, <code>CANCEL_PAYMENT</code>) come back with <code>Success: true</code> through the <strong>same</strong> <code>*-reply</code> topics, and <code>processReply</code> advances by the topic. By the reading, after the saga becomes <code>FAILED</code>, the <code>CANCEL_ORDER</code> reply on <code>pedidos-reply</code> makes the orchestrator send <code>RESERVE_STOCK</code> <strong>again</strong>, and the <code>RELEASE_STOCK</code> one on <code>estoque-reply</code> sends <code>PROCESS_PAYMENT</code>. The saga can reach <code>COMPLETED</code>: it charges and schedules delivery for a canceled order.</li>
  <li><strong><code>FAILED</code> doesn't mean "compensated".</strong> Lines 420 to 428 fire the compensations without checking the return and line 432 writes <code>FAILED</code> <strong>before</strong> any reply. <code>FAILED</code> means "I asked to compensate".</li>
  <li><strong>The delivery is never canceled.</strong> The <code>entregas</code> service implements <code>CANCEL_DELIVERY</code>, but the orchestrator <strong>never</strong> sends it. Since delivery is the last step, it isn't missed today; if a step is added after it, it will be.</li>
</ol>

<p>The fix: the reply carries the <strong>command type</strong> (<code>command_type</code>) and the orchestrator only advances when the current state is the expected one, the conditional transition from lesson 6.</p>

<WhyItMatters>
  <li><strong>Reverse order, only what happened.</strong> Stock failure: 2 compensations; delivery: 3.</li>
  <li><strong>Idempotent by key.</strong> <code>estorno-c-7732</code> repeated refunds only once.</li>
  <li><strong>Repeatable, but finite.</strong> 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 s add up to 31 s; after that it's <code>INTERVENCAO</code> and a person.</li>
  <li><strong>Not everything can be undone.</strong> The PSP fee and the email that was sent stay, and the compensation is another act.</li>
</WhyItMatters>

<Deep min={5}>
  <h4 class="deep-h">One state for each compensation?</h4>
  <p>The store's machine has a single <code>ESTORNANDO</code>. On a delivery failure, three compensations run in a row (refund, release, cancel), and a finer machine would have a state for each one (<code>LIBERANDO</code>, for example). The store keeps 11 states so the text fits in your head.</p>
  <h4 class="deep-h">Correction to the base article: compensation is not an exact undo</h4>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Compensation always possible?</strong> The article treats it that way. It can be <strong>another transaction</strong>, it may <strong>not give everything back</strong>, it has to be <strong>idempotent and repeatable</strong> and sometimes it fails, and then a person sorts it out.</li>
  </ul>
